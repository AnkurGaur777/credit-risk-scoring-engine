# Results

> **Read this first:** every number below comes from the **synthetic stand-in dataset** generated by `credit_risk.synthetic` (same columns and quirks as Home Credit, but fake people). It proves the pipeline works end to end; it says nothing about real borrowers. Re-run on the real Kaggle data (`CR_DATA_SOURCE=real`) and regenerate these documents with `make docs` before quoting any figure.
